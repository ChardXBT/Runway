# Product

LeeWay turns a one-time local archive of Qlob image Community posts into an inspectable style
profile, retrieval index, candidate-ranking pipeline, and ten-day human approval queue. It is
single-user and single-channel today while retaining `channel_id` on core records.

The interface is organized as an editorial desk rather than a generic analytics dashboard. Its
primary surface is a ten-frame queue runway; review keeps the original image, square preview,
caption choices, evidence, provenance, and warnings in one decision flow. The archive exposes all
771 captured records through filtered 60-record pages, while Profile and Activity preserve the
evidence behind suggestions and local state changes.

Out of scope: comments, moderation, credential storage, recurring capture, automated rights
decisions, and live YouTube publishing.
